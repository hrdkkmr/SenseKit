The four modules

Four sensors. Four screenings. One kit.

Each is a full, standalone app with its own AI coach, baseline tracking, monthly report and doctor referral.

🚶
Movement · IMU + Camera

MotionSense

Gait, balance, left–right symmetry & fatigue from the accelerometer, gyroscope and real MoveNet pose tracking. Injury-prevention & fall-risk screening.

Gait & asymmetryBalanceMoveNet poseFall risk
📱 Sensors: accelerometer · gyroscope · camera (pose)
🎤
Speech · Mic + TTS

SpeakSense

A speech-therapy coach (pitch, loudness, clarity in real time) plus an assistive voice for people who can’t speak. Adapts to the user’s own voice.

Therapy coachAssistive voiceClarity scoreSpectrogram
🎙️ Sensors: microphone (Web Audio) · speech recognition · TTS
👂
Hearing · Speaker + Mic

HearSense

Calibration-free digits-in-noise screening + a pure-tone sweep with an audiogram. A room-noise gate keeps results honest. WHO-style method.

Digits-in-noisePure-toneAudiogramNoise gate
🔊 Sensors: speaker (Web Audio tones) · microphone (noise gate)
👁️
Vision · Screen + Camera

VisionSense

Visual-acuity screening, an Ishihara-style colour-blindness test and an Amsler grid — plus daily-life colour tools (namer + Daltonize) for colour-blind users.

AcuityColour blindnessAmsler gridDaltonize
📷 Sensors: screen (optotypes) · camera (distance) · touch
The SenseKit thesis

One engine behind every module.

Every module follows the same proven pattern — which is why they feel like one product, not four demos.

1

Sense

A short test drives a real phone sensor — IMU, mic, speaker or camera.

2

Baseline

Your first good result becomes your personal reference; drift is tracked over time.

3

Coach

An on-device rule-based AI turns numbers into plain-language guidance + exercises.

4

Act

Monthly reports, printable PDFs, and one-tap referral to the right specialist.
