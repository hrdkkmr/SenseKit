The problem

Two groups the system leaves behind.

People who can’t speak clearly (post-stroke dysarthria, apraxia, kids with articulation disorders) need frequent therapy — but there aren’t enough speech-language pathologists. People who can’t speak at all rely on slow or costly assistive tools.

1 in 12

How it works

Speak. Get scored. Improve.

Pick a target sound or word, say it, and SpeakSense gives instant visual feedback that adapts to your voice.

1

Listen

The mic captures your attempt; the app shows a live waveform and spectrogram.

2

Score

On-device analysis measures pitch, loudness, duration, steadiness and a clarity/similarity score.

3

Adapt

SpeakSense learns your baseline and personalises the difficulty and target words over time.

4

Coach

An on-device AI turns the numbers into kind, specific feedback and a daily plan.

Example feedback
“Say ‘water’ — your /r/ was clear, hold the vowel 0.4 s longer.”
“Loudness 62 dB · pitch steady · clarity 78/100 (↑6 this week).”
“3-day streak · /s/ blends improved 12%. Nice work.”
Two modes, one app

Coach the voice you have. Or become the voice you need.

🎤 Therapy Coach

For impaired-but-present speech: dysarthria, apraxia, articulation practice.

  • Real-time pitch, loudness, duration & clarity scoring
  • Personalised, speaker-adaptive targets
  • Corrective drills + progress tracking per sound
  • Clinician-shareable reports (SLP amplifier, not replacement)

🗣️ Assistive Voice

For those who can’t speak: tap words/symbols and the phone speaks for you.

  • Fast phrase board + smart next-word prediction
  • Natural text-to-speech in your chosen voice
  • Save your own quick phrases
  • Works offline, on-device
