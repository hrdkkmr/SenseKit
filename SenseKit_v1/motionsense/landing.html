The problem

You notice a movement problem only after it hurts.

Gait asymmetry, declining balance and reduced steadiness quietly build up — then show up as pain, injury or a fall. Clinical gait & balance labs cost tens of thousands and need specialists.

How it works

A 20-second test. A lifetime of trend.

Pick a test, move naturally, and MotionSense builds your personal movement baseline — then flags drift over time.

1

Move

Do a short walk, balance hold or Timed-Up-&-Go while the phone senses you.

2

Sense

Accelerometer + gyroscope capture gait & sway; the camera runs MoveNet pose tracking for posture & symmetry.

3

Baseline

Your first good session becomes your personal reference. Every future test is compared to it.

4

Coach

An on-device AI explains your numbers, builds a corrective exercise plan, and flags when to see a professional.

Example output
“Left–right gait asymmetry: 8%”
“Trunk stability drops after 10 min — a fatigue signature.”
“Balance worsened 12% vs your baseline.”
“Cadence 164 spm · stride consistency: good.”
