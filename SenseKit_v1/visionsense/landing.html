The problem

People notice blurry vision only after it’s worsened.

Eye tests aren’t always convenient, and colour-blindness often goes undetected for years — affecting school, work and daily life. The phone can screen for both and track change over time.

2.2 billion

people have a vision impairment; nearly half preventable/uncorrected (WHO).

How it works

A two-minute screen. A trend you can track.

1

Distance-aware

The front camera estimates how far the phone is from your face, so letter sizes map to a true visual angle.

2

Acuity test

Tumbling-C / Landolt optotypes shrink with a staircase; you swipe the direction — giving a logMAR estimate.

3

Colour & Amsler

An Ishihara-style plate test screens colour vision; an Amsler grid checks central-vision distortion.

4

Track & assist

Store a baseline, monitor change, and use the colour-namer + Daltonize tools for daily life.

For colour-blind users

We can’t cure it — but we make daily life easier.

Colour blindness is genetic and not curable. VisionSense helps you live with it: identify colours instantly and re-shade the world so you can tell them apart.

🎯

Colour namer

Point the camera (or tap the screen) and VisionSense tells you the colour name — great for clothes, wires, charts and signals.

🌈

Daltonize filter

Re-maps colours so red-green or blue-yellow differences become distinguishable — a live assistive filter for images and the screen.
